Research Projects

Oct. 2025 – Present
Lead Research Scientist, African Research Academy for Women (ARA-W) — Revna Biosciences Labs, Accra, Ghana

Project Title: Evaluation of cfDNA Concentrations in CA125 and PSA Serum Samples for Cancer Progression Analysis

Supervisors: Dr. Derrick Akpalu; Ms. Nutifafa Patience Senaya

  • Processed 60 clotted blood samples (30 female, 30 male): serum separation by centrifugation, pre-analytics logged and tracked.
  • Ran chemiluminescent immunoassays on Maglumi 800 to quantify CA125 and PSA; classified results against clinical reference ranges.
  • Isolated circulating cell-free DNA (cfDNA) from serum using the MagCore automated system; verified yields and purity.
  • Quantified cfDNA via Qubit dsDNA HS assay; confirmed instrument readouts and workflow using MagCore (cartridge 115) where applicable.
  • Performed gel electrophoresis to assess cfNA fragment profiles; computed percent cfNA and evaluated sample integrity.
  • Analyzed associations between cfDNA and biomarker levels using R (Spearman correlation, Mann–Whitney U), generating figures and summary tables for reporting.

Oct. 2025 – Present
Assistant Research Scientist, Histopathology Lab — Revna Biosciences, Accra, Ghana

Project Title: Comparative Evaluation of Rapid Diagnostic Test (RDT) Brands for Serological Screening of Infectious Diseases

Supervisor: Dr. Faith Asasekansa

  • Designed and executed head-to-head evaluations of commercial RDTs for HIV, HBsAg, HCV, Syphilis, and Tuberculosis under standardized serology workflows.
  • Measured diagnostic performance (sensitivity, specificity, accuracy) against reference methods; compiled ROC metrics and lot-to-lot checks.
  • Summarized findings into recommendations on kit selection, usability, and cost-effectiveness for clinical screening programs.

Jan. 2025 – Sept. 2025
Undergraduate Thesis Project — Lead Student Researcher, The Ohui Owusu Lab, Department of Theoretical and Applied Biology, KNUST

Project Title: Antibiotic Resistance of E. coli in Domestic Fowl and Poultry Fowl

  • Conducted a microbiological investigation into the patterns of antibiotic resistance in Escherichia coli strains isolated from domestic and poultry-reared birds.
  • Utilized biochemical tests (catalase, oxidase, citrate, indole) to identify and characterize isolates by enzymatic and metabolic properties.
  • Applied Kirby–Bauer disk diffusion to assess antimicrobial susceptibility profiles to commonly used antibiotics.
  • Measured and analyzed inhibition zones following CLSI guidelines to determine resistance categories.
  • Produced evidence to inform antibiotic stewardship and assess zoonotic risks in poultry farming.

Jul. 2024
Scientific Research Capstone Project — Student Investigator, Department of Theoretical and Applied Biology, KNUST

Project Title: Impacts of Mercury Exposure on Human Health

  • Reviewed epidemiological literature on mercury exposure in artisanal gold mining communities and synthesized human health outcomes.
  • Interpreted a cross-sectional dataset of miners to link exposure indicators with symptoms (neurological, dermatological) using χ² and bivariate analyses.
  • Outlined interventions: public health education, PPE training, and environmental controls; presented findings in a graded poster and oral defense.

Feb. 2023
Environmental Microbiology Research Assistant, L. A. Ofori Research Group, Department of Theoretical and Applied Biology, KNUST

Project Title: Ubiquitous Presence of Bacteria: An Environmental Exploration

  • Prepared sterile media and plates (Nutrient Agar, Potato Dextrose Agar); sanitized equipment and work surfaces with 70% ethanol.
  • Sampled lab air, outdoor air, soil, and plant surfaces; incubated plates and documented growth dynamics after 24–48 hours.
  • Observed widespread microbial presence; noted taxa consistent with Actinomyces, Staphylococcus, and Aspergillus; maintained records for future studies.
